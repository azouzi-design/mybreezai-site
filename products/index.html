AI Meal Planner & Inbox Picks

Personalized recipes, wherever your shoppers are.

On-demand meal planning on your site, plus smart recommendations delivered straight to their inbox, automatically.

What it does

  • Generates personalized recipes on demand, built around each shopper's preferences, lifestyle, budget, and household needs
  • Sends the same kind of personalized picks straight to their inbox, meal plans, replenishment reminders, and offers, without any manual work from your team
  • Surfaces targeted coupons automatically, both on-site and in email, found on the ingredients shoppers actually need
  • Learns preferences once and applies them everywhere, on your site and in their inbox alike

Why it matters to you

Shoppers already expect recipe ideas and personalization when they shop online, and grocers who deliver it, wherever the shopper happens to be, earn more repeat visits. AI Meal Planner + Inbox Picks keeps that personalization going even when a shopper isn't actively on your site, so you're staying in front of them without adding work for your team.

Chat & Click

Give shoppers real answers, without needing to replace your website or app.

A conversational AI assistant that helps shoppers search, build a basket, and shop around their own preferences and allergies.

What it does

  • Lets shoppers search naturally ("what pairs well with salmon?" or "what's on sale this week in the cereal aisle?") and get real, in-stock products back – not generic search results
  • Adds items directly to a shopper's basket from chat
  • Remembers preferences and allergies shoppers have already shared
  • Leverages data you already have – T-logs, coupons, circulars – to dynamically build and refresh each shopper's experience, with no effort from you
  • Deploys on top of your current storefront, no rip-and-replace required

Why it matters to you

If you're not ready to replace your storefront, or you're locked into a contract with another provider, Chat and Click still gets you real personalization now. It works with your existing inventory and ordering systems, so shoppers get help finding what they need without you changing anything else.

Smart Store

A digital storefront that updates itself, so you don't have to.

A dynamic, self-managed digital storefront built for grocers who don't have the time or team to keep their site current.

What it does

  • Automatically updates inventory, promotions, and recommendations by integrating with your transaction logs and circulars, no manual maintenance
  • Personalizes every visit: restock recommendations based on real purchase history, trending items pulled from actual store sales, new-this-week picks, and coupons matched to what each shopper actually buys
  • Available backend to add your own twist, goods, and offers, if you wish to
  • No two shoppers see the same storefront
  • Coming Q1 2027: Adding pick-to-order for pickup

Why it matters to you

Most independent storefronts look the way they do because there's no time to manage them, so the job gets outsourced or ignored. Smart Store removes that burden entirely, built to run itself, while giving every shopper a personalized experience the way national chains already do.
