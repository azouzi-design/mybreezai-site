Our Mission

Empowering independent grocers to thrive as AI reshapes retail

Independent grocers know their shoppers better than anyone: by name, by habit, by what goes in the cart every week. That knowledge has always lived in the store, while the chains use AI to do the same thing online, for millions of shoppers at once. EVERYAISLE exists to close that distance, giving independent grocers that same technology under their own name, so the relationships they have spent generations building carry into every aisle.

From Our Founder

Why we started EVERYAISLE

We saw an opportunity to simplify and improve how people plan and shop for meals – and a chance to help the grocers who serve them do it better.

Our desire was to create a solution that combines personalization, convenience, and value. We believed that AI could fundamentally enhance the grocery shopping experience for everyone involved.

But more than that, we wanted to provide opportunities for regional and independent grocers to compete and thrive – not just survive – in a rapidly changing market.

EVERYAISLE exists to level the playing field. To give every grocer the tools to deliver experiences that keep customers coming back.
