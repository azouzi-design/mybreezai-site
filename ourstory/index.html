Our Mission

Empowering independent grocers to thrive in the AI era

EVERYAISLE builds AI-powered shopping technology for regional and independent grocers, helping them grow basket size, deepen customer loyalty, and capture retail media revenue that has historically gone to the largest chains. The platform's flexible, white-label technology integrates directly into retailer ecosystems, connecting personalized meal planning, digital savings, and loyalty data into a seamless shopping experience.

From Our Founder

Why I started EVERYAISLE

I saw an opportunity to simplify and improve how people plan and shop for meals – and a chance to help the grocers who serve them do it better.

My desire was to create a solution that combines personalization, convenience, and value. I believed that AI could fundamentally enhance the grocery shopping experience for everyone involved.

But more than that, I wanted to provide opportunities for regional and independent grocers to compete and thrive – not just survive – in a rapidly changing market.

EVERYAISLE exists to level the playing field. To give every grocer the tools to deliver experiences that keep customers coming back.
