Key Capabilities

Everything you need for stress-free meals

SmartMeals makes meal planning and grocery shopping simpler, more affordable, and far less stressful.

Personalized Meal Plans

As Shoppers share allergies, health goals, budget, family size, cooking style, SmartMeals learns, remembers and refines with every interaction. The experience gets better the more a shopper uses it.

Live Inventory Integration

Every recipe uses only products currently available at the shopper's store. No dead ends. No frustration. No reason to shop somewhere else.

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

Each recipe includes clear cooking instructions with dynamic ingredient scaling, so a recipe for two adjusts cleanly to feed six.

Coupons and Deals Built In

SmartMeals surfaces relevant digital coupons and loyalty offers alongside recipes, making it easy for shoppers to save and for your store to drive redemption.

Food is Medicine

SmartMeals can generate recipes aligned to specific health conditions like diabetes, high blood pressure, or food allergies, helping shoppers eat better without the guesswork.

Budget-Aware Planning

Shoppers can set a weekly meal budget and SmartMeals will plan around it, surfacing deals and coupons from your loyalty program to help them stretch every dollar at your store.
